Bookings & weather

A change of plan.

What to know about changes, cancellations and rainy days.

Booking changes

To change your time, contact us at least 72 hours before your booking. One change is allowed, subject to court availability.

Cancellations

Bookings are non-refundable. Unused sessions cannot be carried forward.

Rain-affected games

If rain interrupts a session of less than 35 minutes, contact us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of your original booking. Your game can be rescheduled within one month, subject to availability.
