Limited free parking at the entrance. {{ business.parking.name }} faces the main road: {{ business.currency }}{{ business.parking.price }} flat rate on weekdays after 5pm and weekends all day. Validate at the counter.

What happens if I need to change my booking?

No refunds or carry-forward for unused sessions. Give 72 hours’ notice to change a booking; subject to availability and limited to one change.

What if rain interrupts the game?

Request rescheduling within 48 hours of the original booking via WhatsApp. Rain-affected sessions under 35 minutes can be moved to a time within one month of the original date, subject to availability.

Can children visit?

Children aged 12 and below must be supervised by an adult responsible for their safety and behaviour.
